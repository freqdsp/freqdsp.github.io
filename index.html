Install on Mac

  1. Download the installer and open it.
  2. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

Prism comes as Audio Unit, VST3 and Standalone, for macOS 12 or later. Need help? Read the manual.
